The effort

~116 mi, +7,600 ft over 3.5 days. Short Friday, then 35–50 mi gravel days, one 5,600 ft pass, a rough hiker/biker stretch.

The commitment

No cell service, dispersed camping, grizzly country. Warm days, near-freezing nights up high. solitude, Polebridge bear claws, alpine lakes, a Lake McDonald swim.

Getting there & gear

Delta 2575, MSP → Kalispell. Bike + bags rent in Whitefish; bear spray we buy locally before rolling. Bring riding kit, layers, a shell. Monday hotel included from my work before you fly back Tuesday.

Ballpark cost

Bike + bags are roughly $275 for four rental days before bear spray, food, and misc gear.

Know Before You Go

Camping

All first-come — no reservations anywhere. Hiker/biker sites rarely fill.

Park entry

No vehicle reservations required anywhere in 2026. Cyclists still need a Glacier entrance pass: $20/person, valid 7 days.

Fire

August fire bans are common — plan no-cook or a canister stove.

Bears

Grizzly country. Carry spray, make noise on blind corners.
